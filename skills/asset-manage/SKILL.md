# Manage Assets

## Scope

Use this skill when a project-produced image, infographic, report, or other
non-source asset needs active storage. It receives the asset, its purpose,
type, provenance, and documentation relationship, and produces a correctly
located asset plus factual references.
Primary agent role: documentation-agent.

## Workflow

1. Read the plan and confirm the asset's purpose, source, type, and project relevance.
2. Store images and infographics in `assets/images/`, reports in `assets/reports/`, and another type in a purpose-named folder.
3. Add only factual captions or references, then refresh affected INFO navigation.
4. Escalate unclear, unverified, externally sourced, or publication-sensitive assets.

## Constraints

- Do not treat an asset as QA evidence or an unsupported product claim.
- Do not publish, delete, or replace assets without explicit authorization.
