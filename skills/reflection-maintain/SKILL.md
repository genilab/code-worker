# Maintain Reflection

## Scope

Use this skill when a release or material decision needs a durable reflection.
It produces an evidence-backed record of decisions, human checkpoints,
limitations, and lessons.
Primary agent role: documentation-agent.

## Workflow

1. Read the relevant decision, QA, and release records.
2. Record factual outcomes and unresolved lessons.
3. Link to evidence without repeating or inventing it.

## Constraints

- Do not use reflection to approve a release or hide limitations.
